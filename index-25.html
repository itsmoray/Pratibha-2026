The seven houses

Every student belongs to a house drawn from their programme. Points from your events add to your house's tally across the fest.

Before you register

The essentials from the official guidelines — read the full rules for your specific event on its card above.

Five events, your choice of split

Pick either 3 off-stage + 2 on-stage, or 2 off-stage + 3 on-stage. Short film doesn't count toward this limit.

No spot entries

Individual and group events must be registered in advance. Late registrations are not accepted on the day.

Two on-stage group events max

You can lead or join at most two on-stage group events, and each house may field only two teams per group event.

Only the team leader registers

For group events, one person registers for the whole team and lists everyone's name and batch. Multiple entries for the same team cause disqualification.

Consult your house coordinator

Every event except off-stage literary events should be registered only after checking with your house faculty or student coordinator.

College email only

Registration is open only to students with an official CVV email address ending in @cvv.ac.in.

Off-stage events
28–29 September. Choose one language per writing event, or mark it not interested.

Poetry writing

Original composition, written during the hour. Pick a language or skip.

Short story writing

Original fiction, 600–800 words. Pick a language or skip.

Essay writing

800–1000 words on a topic given at the start. Pick a language or skip.

Fine arts event

One fine-arts event only — water colouring, pencil drawing, clay modelling, rangoli, mehendi, collage or photography.

Short film

Under 2 minutes, theme “The Rhythm of Sapta Swaras”. Only the director registers. Doesn't count toward your 5-event limit. Submission deadline: 28 September, 5:00 PM.
